# Prompt induk (gaya) untuk ChatGPT

Dipakai otomatis di dalam setiap berkas prompt. Berkas ini hanya acuan bila kamu ingin mengubah gaya.

## A. Gaya pixel (lokasi, peta)
```
Pixel art top-down RPG Maker style 16-bit scene of a small Indonesian town, bright saturated colors, clean dark outlines, tiled buildings and props, tropical warm light. Generic buildings only, no logos, no real brand names, no readable text, no watermark. Consistent palette.
```

## B. Gaya karakter novel (potret, adegan, halaman pembuka)
```
Anime visual novel illustration, soft cel-shading, glossy detailed eyes, clean lineart, warm natural lighting. Wholesome, adult characters in modest everyday Indonesian clothing (unless stated as a child), natural friendly proportions. Background is a pixel-art Indonesian small town scene, slightly blurred. Do not include logos, real brand names, readable text, or watermarks. Consistent style across the whole series.
```

## C. Larangan umum
```
Do not include any sexualized poses or clothing, likeness of real people, brand logos, watermarks, readable text, or distorted hands.
```

Mengubah gaya: ganti teks A/B di semua berkas dengan pencarian dan penggantian, lalu buat ulang gambar.
